Dependence on existing relationships

Referrals, word of mouth, content and trade shows can generate valuable business. However, relying on these channels makes future opportunities less predictable and harder to plan for.

Limited time for prospecting

Founders and sales teams need to focus on commercial conversations. Market research, contact identification, outreach and follow-ups often take time away from winning new business.

Tools without a working process

AI tools, databases and CRMs can support growth. Without a structured process, however, research, outreach and qualification often fail to deliver consistent results.

Your business development function, operated by us

We guarantee accepted opportunities, not meetings booked.

Our business development engagements are structured around qualified opportunities your team considers commercially worth pursuing. A booking alone does not count toward delivery.

01

Commercial assessment

Review your offering, customer value, margins, sales cycle and delivery capacity. Assess growth constraints and the commercial case for adding another source of demand.

02

Buyer and market definition

Define the target companies, relevant business situations and key decision-makers. Establish the needs you can address, accounts outside your scope and the criteria for a qualified opportunity.

03

Ongoing intelligence

Use company records and market signals across hiring, funding, debt, expansion, announcements and leadership changes. Relate these developments to your offering and assess their potential relevance.

04

Positioning and outreach

Create messaging based on the buyer's business and a relevant reason to connect. Manage targeted email outreach and follow-ups, supported by company research and a clear commercial purpose for each approach.

05

Response review and qualification

Review responses against the agreed criteria, establish context and arrange relevant discussions. Your team evaluates each opportunity against the acceptance criteria once the conversation has taken place.

06

Feedback and improvement

Review segment performance, accepted conversations and reasons for rejection. Use these findings to improve account selection, messaging and follow-up, focusing on meaningful outcomes rather than activity alone.
