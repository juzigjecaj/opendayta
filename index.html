How the process works

The lab licenses data generated through your company’s everyday operations.

Step 1

Discovery & pricing

Identifying licensable datasets across your business and tailor an offer based on the nature, scale, and value of your data.

Step 2

Contracting

Establishing licensing terms & a commitment not to distribute your data until identifying info has been anonymized.

step 3

Delivery & acceptance

You securely transfer your data through APIs or other approved methods. Labs review the data for completeness.

step 4

Payment

Pay in full within 30 days of acceptance, with payments typically ranging from $100k to $3M+.
