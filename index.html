FAQ

Frequent questions, answered.

Frequent questions, answered.

01

What do labs offer companies in exchange for their data?

Labs use real-world business workflows and operational knowledge to improve AI systems. Participating companies receive compensation for their data and may benefit from improvements to the AI use cases they support.

02

What kinds of companies are eligible for the program?

We're looking for operationally mature companies with 30+ employees, established documentation, and quality data across SOPs, knowledge bases, CRM systems, project histories, and internal processes. US companies are currently prioritized, followed by other Western markets.

03

How is company data protected?

Customer data is protected through PII removal, restricted access, and isolated processing pipelines. Data is retained only for agreed periods, with sample reviews available before use and deletion requests honored under the engagement terms.

04

Who owns the data?

Companies retain full ownership of their underlying data throughout the partnership. Sharing data with the lab does not transfer ownership or grant permanent rights to the original records. All data usage is limited to the agreed purposes and terms of the engagement.

05

When will my company get paid?

Once your company transfers its data, the lab has 30 days to review and approve it. After the data is approved, we'll ask your company to submit an invoice, and payment will be made within 30 days of invoicing.

06

What happens to the datasets after they are shared?

Data is processed according to agreed use cases and handling requirements. PII may be removed, and datasets may be synthetically rewritten to preserve workflow structure while protecting original records. Companies can review samples before use, data is retained only for agreed periods and deleted at the end of the engagement.
