How the process works

The lab licenses data generated through your company’s everyday operations.

1
Discovery & pricing

We identify licensable datasets across your business and tailor an offer based on the nature, scale, and value of your data.

2
Contracting

We establish licensing terms, including a commitment not to resell or distribute your data until identifying information has been anonymized.

3
Delivery & acceptance

You securely transfer your data through our APIs or other approved methods. We review the data for completeness.

4
Payment

We pay in full within 30 days of acceptance, with payments typically ranging from $150K to over $2M.
