We guarantee accepted opportunities, not meetings booked.

Business development focuses on finding new customers. Deal origination focuses on identifying companies that may fit an acquisition, advisory mandate or financing opportunity. The intended outcome defines the research criteria and what qualifies as an accepted opportunity.

Private equity and sponsors

Company research guided by your acquisition criteria, including industry, geography, business model and strategic fit. Potential platform and add-on targets are mapped, relevant owners and decision-makers identified, and opportunities for an initial discussion assessed. Your team selects targets and manages the acquisition process.

M&A advisers

Company-side sourcing for potential mandates aligned with your advisory practice. Research focuses on your areas of expertise, the businesses you serve and the circumstances that may warrant an initial discussion. Leadership changes and expansion can guide the research but do not confirm an owner's intention to sell.

Lenders

Research based on your lending criteria, including borrower profile, funding needs, asset coverage, geography and exclusions. Company activity helps identify potential financing needs, while credit assessment and lending decisions remain with your team.

From market research to qualified opportunities.

Agree the criteria

Define the target companies, relevant situations, key exclusions and information needed to support an initial discussion. Responsibilities, existing relationships and the process for introducing opportunities to your team are also agreed upfront.

Research the market

Develop a company database using available business information and ongoing market research. Each profile brings together company details and key decision-makers, with open questions identified for further discussion.

Develop conversations

Develop relevant reasons for contacting each segment, coordinate outreach and follow-ups, and assess responses in context. Direct engagement helps establish interest, timing and suitability.

Qualify and learn

Record conversation details and assess their relevance to your objectives. Feedback on suitability, timing and outstanding information guides further research.
